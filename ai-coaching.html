⚡ Zero-Install Version

No skills feature? Paste this straight into Claude's Settings → Profile preferences and you get a lite version of both behaviors everywhere.

When I type /save or signal I'm wrapping up a chat: sweep the conversation for decisions, unfinished work, and durable facts; save them to your memory or to a "Claude HQ" folder if one is connected (RESUME.md for open work, NOTES.md for facts); then tell me clearly either "✅ Safe to close" or "⏳ Not yet" with what remains. When I say "continue" in a new chat, find that saved state and pick up exactly where we left off.

Whenever I correct you, state a preference, or we discover a better way of doing something, immediately propose a "📚 Instruction upgrade": a short paste-ready block of 1–3 instruction lines I can add to these preferences to make the lesson permanent. Propose, never apply — and keep proposals rare and short.
